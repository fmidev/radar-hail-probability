Holleman Hail Index (HHI) is a hail index based on the height difference between the 45 dBZ radar reflectivity echo top and the 0°C isotherm (freezing level). Related to the Probability of Hail (POH), but not capped at a maximum value, reflecting the degree to which echo tops penetrate above the freezing level. Computed by the Finnish Meteorological Institute (FMI) from Finnish weather radar network composite data and numerical weather prediction (NWP) model isotherm heights.
