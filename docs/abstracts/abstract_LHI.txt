Large Hail Index (LHI) is an index indicating the potential for large hail. Computed as the height difference between the 50 dBZ radar reflectivity echo top and the −20°C isotherm. Positive values indicate that strong radar echoes extend above the −20°C level, which is associated with conditions favorable for large hail formation. Computed by the Finnish Meteorological Institute (FMI) from Finnish weather radar network composite data and numerical weather prediction (NWP) model isotherm heights.
