Tuovinen Hail Index (THI) is a hail index developed at the Finnish Meteorological Institute (FMI) as a modification of the Holleman Hail Index (HHI). In addition to the echo-top-based hail index, the Tuovinen Hail Index includes an adjustment based on the absolute height of the 0°C isotherm (freezing level), accounting for the climatological effect of freezing level height on surface hail occurrence. Computed from Finnish weather radar network composite data and numerical weather prediction (NWP) model isotherm heights.
