Probability of Hail (POH) is the probability of hail occurrence at the surface. Estimated from the height difference between the 45 dBZ radar reflectivity echo top and the 0°C isotherm (freezing level), based on empirical relationships derived from hail observations. Computed by the Finnish Meteorological Institute (FMI) from Finnish weather radar network composite data and numerical weather prediction (NWP) model isotherm heights.
